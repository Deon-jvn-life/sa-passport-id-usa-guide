FAQ: South African passports and ID in the USA

Can I walk in at a VFS centre?

No. The SA Chamber summary of the official webinar says there are no walk-in appointments and all appointments are made online [source].

Do I have to use the centre for my state?

No. You can use any of the US centres regardless of your state of residence [source].

Can I mail my renewal to the Embassy?

No. The Embassy says no mailed-in passport, ID or birth registration applications will be processed [source].

How do I pay at VFS?

Credit or debit card only [source].

How much is an adult renewal?

$127 at VFS: $67 consular fee plus $60 VFS service fee. MAXI is $193 [source].

How many photos?

Four colour photos, 35 x 45 mm, for passports and IDs at VFS [source].

My child is 15 and turns 16 this year. Can they renew a passport?

They must apply for a first ID first or together with the first adult passport; both parents and the child must attend [source, source].

I am travelling to South Africa in a few days and my passport is lost.

Contact the Embassy about an Emergency Travel Certificate; minimum processing is 7 business days and VFS does not issue them [source, source].

When should I apply?

VFS recommends booking at least 18 months before expiry; the Embassy suggests 12 months [source, source].

Can I renew my Smart ID card at VFS?

Sources disagree. VFS lists a Smart ID re-issue fee, while DHA and SA Chamber describe the Smart ID card as a later phase. See Smart ID.
