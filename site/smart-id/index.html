South African Smart ID in the USA

First ID (16 and older): VFS takes first ID applications for children aged 15 turning 16 in the year and for anyone 16 or older who never applied for a first ID. Fee $60 (consular $0 + VFS $60) [source, source].

Re-issue: VFS publishes a "Re-Issue ID Card" checklist and a Smart ID re-issue fee of $72 ($12 + $60) [source]. Required: BI-529, DHA-9 at the appointment, certified unabridged birth certificate (and name change approval if relevant), certified unabridged marriage certificate for female applicants, and four photos [source].

Conflicts between official sources (as of 21 Oct 2026)
  • Processing time. VFS says six to eight weeks [source]; the SA Chamber webinar summary says 8 to 12 weeks from receipt by DHA in Pretoria and, elsewhere on the same page, up to 2 to 3 months [source]; the Embassy says a minimum of 6 to 8 months for applications lodged with it [source, source].
  • Smart ID re-issue at VFS. The VFS fee table lists "Smart ID - Reissue" ($12 + $60) [source], but DHA says the centres initially offer passports and birth certificates [source], and the SA Chamber summary lists the Smart ID card as Phase 2 with the date "to be announced later" and First ID as "not re-issue" [source]. Check the VFS booking categories before you book.
  • Washington DC centre. DHA and SA Chamber list a VFS centre in Washington DC [source, source]; the Embassy page lists only Atlanta, Miami, Houston, Chicago, Los Angeles and New York [source].
  • Applying at the Embassy. The Embassy page says "No appointment required" for applications Monday to Thursday, 9:30 a.m. to 12:30 p.m., but also that no mailed-in passport, ID or birth registration applications will be processed and points applicants to VFS [source].
  • How far ahead to apply. VFS says book at least 18 months before your passport expires [source]; the Embassy says 12 months [source].

The Embassy re-issue checklist lists DHA-9, BI-529, copy of passport, marriage certificate if changing surname, 2 photos and a $12 money order, with a minimum 6 to 8 months processing [source]. It also says no mailed-in applications will be processed from 1 March 2026.

Planning a visit home? Some South African banks offer Smart ID applications at branches. See the separate SA Smart ID bank branch finder.
