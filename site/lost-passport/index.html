Lost South African passport in the USA and emergency travel

Replacing a lost or stolen passport

  • Report the loss to the police and complete form DI-47 (loss of passport report); bring both to VFS with your other documents [source].
  • Fees at VFS: lost adult or child passport $194 ($134 + $60); lost MAXI $326 ($266 + $60) [source].
  • The Embassy also publishes a report of lost travel document form [source].

Emergency Travel Certificate (ETC)

VFS does not issue ETCs [source]. The ETC is handled by the Embassy. It is a one-way travel document to South Africa only, valid for a maximum of three months from issue [source].

  • Completed DHA-73 and DHA-9 (a copy of the original DHA-9 can be used; prints must be clear)
  • 2 photos (colour, no smile, mouth closed, no glasses)
  • Copy of passport, SA birth certificate or identity document
  • Proof of travel arrangements and a written reason for urgent travel to South Africa
  • Fee $12 money order payable to SA Embassy, plus a prepaid envelope for collection
  • Minimum processing period 7 business days. Submit or mail to the South African Embassy, 3051 Massachusetts Avenue NW, Washington, DC 20008.
[source]

Home Affairs will not approve an ETC for applicants eligible for a first ID (turning 16 and above) who have not applied for it [source].

DHA says a South African living abroad who still holds South African citizenship needs a valid South African passport to travel to South Africa, and that proof of a passport application is not accepted for travel. See citizenship [source].
