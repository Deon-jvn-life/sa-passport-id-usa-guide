VFS Global centres for South African passports in the USA

All 7 centres listed by DHA on 21 Oct 2026 [source]. Hours are for both submission and collection.

Appointments only at VFS. No walk-ins. The SA Chamber USA summary of the official DHA/DIRCO webinar says there are no walk-in appointments at VFS centres and that all appointments must be made online on the VFS website [source]. Any VFS centre can be used regardless of the state you live in [source, source]. Book only through services.vfsglobal.com/usa/en/zap.
