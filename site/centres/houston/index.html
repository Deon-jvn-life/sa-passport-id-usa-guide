South African passport and ID at VFS Houston

Address: 1001 Texas Ave, Floor 10, Suite 1020, Houston, TX 77002 [source]

Days: Monday to Friday (except public holidays)

Hours: 9:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m. (submission and collection) [source]

Booking: online, through VFS Global for South Africa in the USA. The Department of Home Affairs lists an online booking link for this centre [source, source].

Who can use it: the New York consulate says you may submit at the centre most convenient to you [source].

Services

  • Passport applications and renewals, adult and child [source, source]
  • First identity document for a child turning 16 in the year, or for anyone 16 or older who has not applied for a first identity document [source, source]
  • Birth registration within 30 days of birth. VFS Global does not take late registration. Late registration goes to a South African mission [source]
  • Not on the VFS Global list: Emergency Travel Certificate [source]
Birth registration conflict. VFS Global accepts a birth registration within 30 days of birth [source]. The Department of Home Affairs says a citizen born abroad whose birth is not on the population register cannot be served at these centres and must go to a mission [source].

Before you go

Bring every form and document on the what to bring checklist, and a credit or debit card: VFS accepts card only [source]. See fees.
