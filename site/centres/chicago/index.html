South African passport and ID at VFS Chicago

Address: 142 E Ontario Street, Suite 1700, Chicago, IL 60611 [source]

Days: Monday to Friday (except public holidays)

Hours: 9:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m. (submission and collection) [source, source]

Booking: online only, VFS Global South Africa USA. No walk-ins [source].

Who can use it: any South African citizen or dual citizen in the USA, whatever state you live in [source].

Services

  • Passport applications and renewals, adult and child [source, source]
  • First ID application for those turning 16 in the year, or 16 and older without a first ID [source]
  • Birth registration within 30 days of birth (late registration goes to the Embassy) [source]
  • Not offered: Emergency Travel Certificate [source]

Before you go

Bring every form and document on the what to bring checklist, and a credit or debit card: VFS accepts card only [source]. See fees.
