South African Embassy and consulates in the USA

VFS centres now take most passport, first ID and birth registration applications. The missions still handle emergency travel certificates, late birth registration and other civic matters. The Embassy citizens services page links to the DHA citizenship reinstatement portal. See citizenship [source, source].

Embassy of South Africa, Washington DC

Address: 3051 Massachusetts Avenue NW, Washington, DC 20008

Phone: +1 (240) 937 5760 (main); +1 (202) 413 1953 (emergency)

Email: info.saembassydc@dirco.gov.za

Hours: Applications and collections Monday to Thursday, 9:30 a.m. to 12:30 p.m., no appointment required (as stated on the SA Citizens Services page). Telephone enquiries Tuesday and Thursday, 2:00 p.m. to 4:00 p.m.

Area served: Alabama, Arkansas, District of Columbia, Florida, Georgia, Illinois, Indiana, Iowa, Kansas, Louisiana, Maryland, Michigan, Minnesota, Mississippi, Missouri, Nebraska, North Dakota, South Dakota, Ohio, Oklahoma, Texas, Virginia, Puerto Rico, Virgin Islands and Wisconsin
