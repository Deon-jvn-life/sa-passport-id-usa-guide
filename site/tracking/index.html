Track a South African passport or ID application from the USA

  • VFS tracking: use the "Track your application" option in the menu of the VFS Global South Africa USA site [source]. The address visatracking.vfsglobal.com/Global returned 404 Not Found on 26 Oct 2026, so this guide does not link it.
  • Collection: DHA says that from 1 November 2025 service centres offer optional secure courier delivery to an address you choose [source]. Centre hours include collection times [source].
  • Embassy applications: follow up on status through the Home Affairs contact centre at hacc@dha.gov.za. Send your prepaid collection envelope only once the Embassy tells you the document is ready (postage not less than $10) [source].
  • Los Angeles Consulate: to have a passport returned by post, bring a prepaid self-addressed USPS or UPS envelope; FedEx is not accepted [source].
Expected timing differs by official source: six to eight weeks on the VFS Global page, and at least 6 to 8 months at the Embassy [source, source].
