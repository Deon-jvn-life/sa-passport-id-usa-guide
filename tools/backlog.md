## Child passport at a US mission

Partly covered on `what-to-bring` from the VFS consular page (both parents present under 18, consent from Home Affairs or a mission if a parent is outside the USA, no fingerprints under 15 and a half). Not yet a full page.

Official material not yet written up in full:

- Embassy child passport checklist (February 2026 PDF): DHA-73 signed by the parents, DHA-529, copy of passport, copy of South African unabridged birth certificate, consent letter if divorced or unmarried, 4 photos, $67 money order, minimum 6 to 8 months, no mailed-in applications from 1 March 2026. A single parent may sign only with supporting documents for sole custody.
- Embassy child consent PDF: the case where one parent is abroad and the other is in South Africa with the child. It says 3 working days and gives an affidavit, copies, and the email of the Home Affairs office in South Africa. It is a different process from lodging the child's passport at VFS.
- DHA-73 itself sets out consent of both parents or guardians, the out-of-wedlock rule, death, court orders, and sole guardianship.
- The older Embassy "South African Passport" HTML page still describes child passports, four 2 x 2 photos, and who must sign. It may be older than the February 2026 checklist. Compare them before publishing a merged list.

## Emergency Travel Certificate

Already a section on `lost-passport`, from the Embassy ETC PDF: one-way to South Africa, maximum three months, $12 money order, minimum 7 business days, VFS does not issue it.

## Register a US-born child's birth

Worth a full page. Sources exist and they conflict, which is why it was not the page added this round.

- Embassy birth registration page: citizenship by descent, about 4 months, abridged certificate first, then passport and full certificate. Lists DHA-24 (barcoded, father signs section F if both parents are South African), BI-529 / DHA-529, foreign birth certificate, marriage certificate, proof of the South African parent's citizenship, notarized consent from the non-South African parent or the South African mother, and a certified copy of the non-South African parent's passport or ID. It also says children whose births are registered in South Africa may keep dual citizenship. It still tells people to request the barcoded form by mail.
- Embassy citizens services: no mailed-in birth registration applications, and birth registration is pointed to VFS together with passports and first IDs. DHA-24 cannot be downloaded.
- DHA service centres abroad (already cited on the home page): a citizen born abroad whose birth is not registered cannot be served at the VFS centres and must go to a mission.
- VFS fee table (already on `fees`): birth registration $6 consular + $60 VFS. The centre pages say registration within 30 days, and late registration goes to the Embassy. The VFS page returned 403 to an automated check on 23 October 2026.
- DHA foreign birth registration page lists DHA-24, DHA-529, full birth certificate, and extra items from age 15 (paternity or DNA, interviews, police reports). Fetch the live page again before using the age-15 list. The URL checked was `https://www.dha.gov.za/index.php/10-immigration-services/886-foreign-birth-registration`.
- New York civic forms list DHA-24, DHA-24/1, DHA-24/15 and DHA-288 and say "Call" for several of them. No fee on that list.
- DHA reinstatement FAQ: a child who was never registered travels on a foreign passport subject to visa rules, and foreign birth registration is done at the mission or a local office.

There is no BI-24 on the Embassy birth registration requirements above. The Embassy citizenship-by-descent section does say "Completed BI-24". Do not merge BI-24 and DHA-24 without spelling out which page uses which name.
